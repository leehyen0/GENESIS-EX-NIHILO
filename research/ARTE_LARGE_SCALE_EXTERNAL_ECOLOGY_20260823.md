# ARTE Large-Scale External Ecology Shadow

Canonical parent: `f3840760cfc0b5b7eac1e03b4d0f9874b0d1d6b0` (#104).

This shadow does not add a new repair operator. It moves the external-world developmental frontier from manually screening one public red signal at a time to an executable, fail-closed multi-ecology acquisition and prospective meta-improvement contract.

## Pressure

Recent public-world observations showed that a red CI signal is not automatically cognition evidence:

- a red world can recover on prospective re-observation;
- the same source SHA can be a materially different world when runtime/model/service state is unknown;
- `comments=0` does not imply uncontaminated because a prior PR/commit can expose the root cause or patch;
- a deterministic persistent failure can still be development experience rather than heldout authority.

Therefore external residual acquisition is itself a cognitive/research organ that must be selective before BODY mutation is allowed.

## New executable chain

`External signal`
`→ provenance freeze`
`→ repository-wide contamination gate`
`→ environment reconstructability gate`
`→ stochasticity/repeatability gate`
`→ diverse pre-outcome cohort selection`
`→ prospective re-observation`
`→ semantic persistence gate`
`→ frozen BODY expressibility`
`→ OLD+MORE_COMPUTE complete failure`
`→ world-caused language/morphology pressure`
`→ descendant`
`→ multi-ecology generation ledger`

Public/exposed tasks may create development pressure but do not become heldout promotion authority merely because they are external.

## Large-scale cohort contract

The hidden evaluator constructs 72 candidates across six ecologies:

- software
- interactive
- scientific
- planning
- data-system
- tool-use

It mixes clean/reconstructable candidates with contamination, unresolved external-service state, and unresolved stochasticity controls. Candidate consequences are not inputs to acquisition or cohort ranking.

The scheduler admits only candidates that pass all pre-observation gates and enforces ecology diversity.

## Persistence contract

For deterministic or seeded worlds, at least two independent sufficiently matched executions must reproduce the exact semantic failure. A matched-world PASS blocks a persistent-failure claim. Environment drift and signature drift are classified as unavailable rather than converted into cognition evidence.

Unseeded stochastic worlds require an explicit repeatable contract and higher recurrence support.

## External inexpressibility gate

`CURRENT_BODY_LANGUAGE_INEXPRESSIBLE_ON_EXTERNAL_RESIDUAL` opens only when:

- external residual persistence is established;
- BODY hash is frozen;
- inherited language search is complete;
- inherited candidate count is zero;
- OLD+MORE_COMPUTE runs at least 16 repeats and still yields zero candidates;
- current outcomes were not used for candidate generation;
- post-freeze human structural repairs are zero;
- solution/root-cause leakage is absent.

This gate opens development pressure. Promotion authority remains separately governed by exposure and evaluator independence.

## Multi-ecology meta-acceleration contract

The new prospective ledger measures each parent→descendant transition by **frontier delta**, not accumulated absolute frontier:

`P_g = ΔFrontier_g / (Compute_g + Evidence_g + HumanStructuralIntervention_g)`

A bounded multi-ecology meta-acceleration candidate requires at least three continuous transitions across at least three ecology families, with:

- positive frontier delta in every transition;
- strictly increasing transition productivity;
- nonincreasing human structural intervention;
- retained competence and calibration floors;
- improving meta-improvement ability;
- continuous BODY lineage;
- benchmark-disjoint, verified authority;
- no use of the current hidden outcome to generate the mutation.

Even if all of those conditions pass, the implementation deliberately emits `global_recursive_acceleration=false`. It is a candidate-evidence gate, not a self-certifying ASI claim.

## Boundaries

This shadow does not establish a real clean persistent external residual, a real three-generation external descendant chain, organizationally independent custody, AGI, ASI, physical-world closure, foundation-weight learning, or global recursive acceleration.

Its purpose is to scale the epistemic and causal contract so the next real external worlds can be processed without relaxing the existing authority boundary.
