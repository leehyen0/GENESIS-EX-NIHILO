# ARTE External Research Ingest — Self-Improvement and Frontier Benchmark Ecology
Recorded: 2026-08-23 17:14 KST
Status: EXTERNAL KNOWLEDGE INGEST / NOT YET PROMOTED AS CAUSAL LAW

## Pre-read commitments

H1. Static answer benchmarks are insufficient to pressure active exploration, online world-model revision and self-generated goals.
H2. Systems that improve algorithms/agents will rely on an external evaluator and archive/population rather than unconstrained self-claims.
H3. Real AI-R&D tasks need a distinct research-engineering ecology beyond puzzle solving.
H4. Broad expert benchmarks will expose calibration defects that raw accuracy hides.
H5. Existing self-improvement systems will still leave higher mutation/evaluator/search language substantially human-designed.

These hypotheses are not evidence; the sources below update the design.

## ARC-AGI-3 / ARC Prize
Sources: https://arcprize.org/arc-agi/3 ; https://arcprize.org/competitions/2026/arc-agi-3

Observed: interactive novel environments without instructions; exploration, modeling, goal-setting, planning/execution and experience-driven adaptation are central. Hidden holdout environments reduce memorization value.

ARTE consequence: ARC-AGI-3 becomes a primary pressure for orientation, active information acquisition, self-generated goals and online model revision. Replays/action traces should become ExperienceUnits. Public games are development experience; hidden/rolling evaluation remains the authority gate.

## SWE-bench Verified
Source: https://www.swebench.com/verified.html

Observed: human-validated 500-instance subset from real GitHub issues, with solvability/problem/test quality reviewed.

ARTE consequence: real software-repair ecology for representation, repository navigation, patch generation, testing and tools. Preserve localization failures, wrong hypotheses, tool traces and rollback, not only terminal patch success.

## RE-Bench / METR
Source: https://metr.org/blog/2024-11-22-evaluating-r-d-capabilities-of-llms/

Observed: realistic ML research-engineering environments such as scaling-law fitting and GPU-kernel optimization, with a computer/scoring function/resources and open-ended work.

ARTE consequence: strongest current benchmark family for AI-R&D ability. It pressures experiment design, engineering judgment, training/debugging and long-horizon optimization.

## Humanity's Last Exam
Sources: https://lastexam.ai ; https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09962-4

Observed: 2,500 expert-level multimodal closed-ended academic questions across broad subjects; designed because older benchmarks were saturating. The published evaluation emphasizes both accuracy and calibration gaps and retains private evaluation to detect overfitting.

ARTE consequence: expert breadth/depth and calibration become separate axes. More answers with worse confidence calibration is not uniform improvement.

## AlphaEvolve / Google DeepMind
Sources: https://deepmind.google/blog/alphaevolve-a-gemini-powered-coding-agent-for-designing-advanced-algorithms/ ; https://deepmind.google/blog/alphaevolve-impact/

Observed: LLM proposal generation + automated evaluators + evolutionary program database; whole programs/algorithms evolve and are objectively scored, with reported use across computing/math/science.

ARTE consequence: preserve a population/archive and objective external evaluators, then add ARTE-specific provenance, authority separation, REMOVE/WRONG and descendant causal credit.

## Darwin Gödel Machine
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.22954

Observed: iteratively modifies its coding-agent code, empirically evaluates descendants, maintains a diverse archive and can improve mechanisms used in future coding/self-modification.

ARTE consequence: mutator itself must become a mutation target; temporarily weaker but structurally novel stepping stones may remain in shadow. Benchmark score alone is not enough for promotion.

## DiscoPOP
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.08414

Observed: LLM-driven objective discovery proposed/evaluated previously unknown preference-optimization losses; the best discovered loss transferred to held-out tasks.

ARTE consequence: Foundry can eventually evolve loss functions, routers, learned representations and training procedures as organs. Bounded component discovery must not be confused with global intelligence improvement.

## AI Scientist-v2
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.08066

Observed: end-to-end hypothesis, experiment, analysis and manuscript loop; reduced reliance on human-authored code templates and used progressive agentic tree search.

ARTE consequence: research-program generation must connect to actual experiment execution/evaluation, and research/experiment/evaluator managers should become morphology organs.

## Cross-source synthesis

`creative generator + external evaluator/world + persistent archive/population + experience/replay + descendant mutation` is a practical route to bounded self-improvement.

ARTE retains the stronger invariant:
`generation != evidence != authority != action != outcome != causal credit`.

The open problem is whether the BODY can improve the machinery that generates/evaluates its next BODY, transfer that improvement across different real external ecologies, reduce human structural intervention and preserve the effect across multiple actual descendants.
