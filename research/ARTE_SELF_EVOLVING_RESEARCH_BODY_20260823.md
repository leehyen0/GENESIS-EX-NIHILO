## 0. Purpose

The target is not another cognitive module and not another fixed G-number.

The target is one persistent BODY that:
1. continually re-ingests its historical theory, failures, laws, artifacts, source roots and descendant lineage;
2. acquires new external knowledge with provenance;
3. solves real external problems and benchmarks;
4. converts success and failure into typed causal experience;
5. generates new representations, theories, tools, algorithms, organs, connections, goals, evaluators and eventually mutation/compiler rules;
6. compiles candidate descendant bodies;
7. evaluates them on fresh external tasks under causal controls;
8. preserves viable improvements, fossils harmful ones, and retains diverse stepping stones;
9. improves not only task performance but the machinery that creates the next intelligence;
10. persists this entire developmental lineage without treating checkpoint state as authority.

The governing distinction remains:
`generation != evidence != authority != action != outcome != credit`.

## 1. Diagnosis of the current BODY

The representational ascent through PRs #89–#97 is a real structural progression:
`relation schema -> dataflow path -> cross-domain relation -> latent world relation -> observation basis -> response partition -> measurement equivalence -> comparison functional -> nonlinear relation`.

PR #98 goes one meta-level further by generating a finite composition table and storing its lineage in a more-specific persistent BODY.

However, the current representation-algebra runtime still contains a hard-coded developmental topology: if current generation == 7, open generation 8; after that, `MAX_GENERATION_REACHED`.

The composition-law generator also receives a deeper authored metalanguage: exactly three sign states, middle-state identity, commutativity, a fixed finite-table interpreter, activation-subset interpreter, and evaluator consequence semantics.

Therefore the current BODY can generate content and some operators inside progressively higher bounded languages, but it does not yet generate the developmental language/topology that decides what kind of cognition comes next. That is the decisive architectural ceiling.

## 2. Historical theory is a gene pool, not a constitution

Do not all-activate the old theories and do not let the latest theory erase prior work.

Each historical object is stored as a typed gene with source/provenance, semantic ancestry, executable interpretation(s), consumers, preconditions, falsifiers, positive/negative evidence, REMOVE, WRONG_SWAP, transfer range, cost, existence stage, authority scope, descendants and fossilization reason.

### Bias / B
Old: controlled asymmetry. Higher: learned causal prior over search trajectories, representations, theories, architectures, mutation operators and source choices. Bias is not truth.

### Imaginary / complementary space / Sigma-infinity
Old: preserve unrealized/opposite/complementary possibilities. Higher: non-authoritative population of alternative ontologies, grammars, algorithms, BODY topologies, evaluators and goal systems.

### R / J
Promote from transformations over states to transformations over representations and grammars themselves: invert causal factorization, move object ontology to relation ontology, replace a fixed basis with a generated basis, replace linear comparison by nonlinear relation, replace fixed operator alphabet by generated operation law, and later mutate the interpreter/mutation language itself.

### U
Selection extends from answer/action to `ACT / PROBE / LEARN / MUTATE / FORK / QUOTIENT / SPLIT / DEFER / NULL`.

### Pi
Realization remains evidence-scoped. A candidate topology or theory does not become operational merely because it exists.

### F
Self-reconstruction becomes the descendant compiler: `world residual -> localized mutation -> compiled descendant -> re-verification -> fresh transfer -> retained/fossilized lineage`.

### Chaos
Bounded diversity pressure over theories, algorithms and architectures; archive prevents temporary performance loss from deleting useful stepping stones.

### QuestionField
Promote from question generation to identifiability-pressure generation. It may create a question, measurement, sensor/source request, benchmark, experiment, discriminator, representation coordinate, ontology split, or evaluator mutation request.

### LawGenesis
Operate at multiple levels: `task law -> representation law -> generator law -> architecture law -> mutation law -> evaluator law`. Promotion still requires consumption, REMOVE, semantic WRONG, heldout/source-disjoint transfer, negative-transfer checks, and delayed/restart reconstruction.

### Existence ladder
Apply `BoundaryShadow -> PreobjectPressure -> Originary -> Operational -> Generative -> Transitional -> Identity` to newly generated organs, connections, algorithms, evaluators and architectures.

### Omega
Not an intelligence score. It is a revisable viability/authority/resource governor over frontier, claim scope, compute/evidence budget, retained competence, unresolved debt, diversity, rollback and irreversible-change risk. Omega amendments belong to the highest constitutional mutation level.

## 3. Replace the generation ladder with executable morphology

Represent the BODY as a typed cognitive hypergraph / morphology genome.

A node is an organ: `SOURCE, PERCEPTOR, REPRESENTATION, MEMORY, QUESTION, GENERATOR, PLANNER, TOOL, WORLD, VERIFIER, EVALUATOR, CREDIT, GOAL, COMPILER, MUTATOR, ARCHIVE, GOVERNOR`, plus newly generated types after type genesis is validated.

An edge declares source organ, target organ, artifact/data type, authority requirement, gate, scheduling priority and provenance. The event schedule is also genome state.

The compiler validates the graph and produces a runnable BODY. The mutator edits the graph itself.

Initial mutation vocabulary includes `ADD_ORGAN, REMOVE_ORGAN, REPLACE_ORGAN, ADD_EDGE, REMOVE_EDGE, REWIRE_EDGE, CHANGE_EDGE_TYPE, SET_EVENT_ORDER, SPLIT_ORGAN, MERGE_ORGANS, ADD_SOURCE, ADD_VERIFIER, CHANGE_MEMORY_ROUTE, CHANGE_GOAL_POLICY, CHANGE_GENERATOR, CHANGE_MUTATOR, CHANGE_EVALUATOR, CHANGE_COMPILER, CONSTITUTION_EPOCH`.

This vocabulary is bootstrapping scaffolding, not a claim of unrestricted self-modification. A later frontier must generate/revise this vocabulary itself.

## 4. Split–quotient morphogenesis

Use one general pressure principle across many cognition levels.

### Split
When `same complete frozen phenotype -> different required external outcome` and MORE-COMPUTE within the frozen language cannot resolve it, the BODY may split a state, representation coordinate, relation type, theory family, memory partition, source class, organ, edge semantics, evaluator category or goal category.

### Quotient
When multiple candidates are behaviorally indistinguishable on the frozen support, quotient them instead of counting syntactic multiplicity as intelligence.

### Lift
If a distinction survives source/context shifts, lift it into a reusable law/meta-law.

### Reopen
If fresh transfer breaks the invariant, reopen the quotient or split the scope.

This operationalizes higher dimensionality: dimensions are created only when the current representation causally aliases states that the world requires the system to distinguish.

## 5. Typed causal experience, not chat history

Every meaningful episode becomes an `ExperienceUnit` containing episode ID, pre-BODY hash, source/document/data provenance, task/benchmark ID and contamination state, precommitted hypotheses, uncertainty before/after, goal, representation/theory used, tool/action trace, external outcome, success/failure, mutation IDs, REMOVE, WRONG_SWAP, heldout effect, delayed replay, descendant BODY hash, retained-competence effect, theory update and rollback/fossilization reason.

Memory separates episodic, procedural, structural/architecture, negative/failure, theory genealogy and external-source graph. A memory is valuable only if retrieval beneficially changes future behavior on fresh tasks; storage volume is not cognition growth.

## 6. External knowledge assimilation loop

External knowledge is not copied into a note and called learning.

1. detect identifiability deficit / theory blindspot / task failure;
2. generate a source-query or evidence-source request;
3. before reading, freeze current prediction/questions and uncertainty;
4. acquire paper/document/data with provenance;
5. extract claims, relations, assumptions, uncertainty and contradictions;
6. bind claims to candidate theory genes without immediate authorization;
7. derive a probe, experiment, benchmark task or predicted behavior change;
8. apply on a source-disjoint task;
9. REMOVE and wrong-source/wrong-theory substitution;
10. delayed reconstruction;
11. only then compile procedural/theory update.

Source selection itself is mutable: `IdentifiabilityDeficit -> EvidenceSourceGenesis / EvidenceSourceRequest`.

## 8. Benchmark contamination discipline

Maintain `PUBLIC_TRAINING_FIELD / PUBLIC_DEV_FIELD / FROZEN_HELDOUT / ROLLING_OR_PRIVATE_EXTERNAL / SOURCE_DISJOINT_TRANSFER`.

Record source URLs/hashes, whether answers/patches were exposed, whether a task affected mutation design and whether evaluator feedback was seen. A public-score improvement alone is not proof of recursive self-improvement.

## 9. Pressure-driven autonomous goals

Do not encode one scalar “become superintelligent” objective.

Goal utility includes expected frontier gain + information gain + transfer breadth + meta-improvement potential + novelty/diversity value - compute/evidence cost - irreversibility - survival risk.

## 10. Population and archive, not a single hill climb

Maintain an active safe incumbent, Pareto archive, shadow variants, fossils, temporarily worse but structurally novel stepping stones and exact ancestry.

Select on external capability, transfer, calibration, research novelty, theory/tool invention, efficiency, meta-improvement ability, retained competence, reproducibility and human structural intervention.

## 11. Intelligence-improvement intelligence

Separate ordinary intelligence from the ability to improve intelligence.

Task intelligence: solve unseen tasks, acquire/use new knowledge, adapt online, plan/execute, calibrate uncertainty.

Meta-intelligence: localize why the BODY failed, invent useful representation/theory/tool, propose and compile a BODY mutation, predict its effect before evaluation, improve generator/mutator/evaluator/source selection, reduce human structural edits, and transfer improvement to a different problem family.

The key target is `better BODY -> better mechanism for making the next BODY -> larger/faster future frontier`.

## 12. Prospective recursive-acceleration criterion

Let `C_g` = external heldout capability frontier, `T_g` = cross-domain transfer, `K_g` = retained competence, `Q_g` = calibration, `R_g` = research/invention, `A_g` = architecture/self-improvement ability, `E_g` = evidence/experiment cost, `P_g` = compute/search cost, `H_g` = human structural intervention.

`M_g = DeltaFrontier_g / (E_g + P_g + H_g)`.

Do not call recursive acceleration unless, prospectively across at least three actual parent->descendant generations: frontier expands; `M_(g+1) > M_g`; H is non-increasing; retained competence/calibration remain viable; descendants improve mutation/evaluation/memory/goal machinery; REMOVE of inherited meta-mutation damages next-generation improvement; WRONG_SWAP damages it; source/benchmark-disjoint transfer survives; delayed restart reconstructs the lineage.

## 13. Mutation-depth ladder — a risk classification, not a cognition-family ladder

L0 strategy/search allocation
L1 representation/memory/tool
L2 organ/edge/schedule topology
L3 generator/mutator
L4 evaluator/benchmark/source selection
L5 goal/Omega governance

These levels need not be traversed sequentially. Higher levels require stronger evidence, disjoint evaluation, retained-competence checks and rollback guarantees. The constitutional kernel is amendable but not self-authorizing.

## 14. Survival and non-volatility

Every promoted descendant preserves append-only source roots, negative evidence, failed mutations, law scopes, contamination ledger, exact parent hash, exact mutation set, evaluator/version hashes, archive, rollback target and reconstruction tests.

Authority is never serialized as a trusted boolean. Evidence must be reverified after restore. Canary/retained-competence suites run before promotion. Irreversible changes remain shadow until reversible evidence exists.
