# Evaluation

## Adaptive cognition bounded gates

The adaptive cognition runtime is evaluated as an executable control layer, not as a claim of AGI/ASI.

Required invariants:
1. Easy tasks do not activate all cognition modules.
2. QuestionField prefers higher expected information gain under cost.
3. BoundaryShadow triggers representation escape.
4. Imaginary possibilities remain non-asserted query targets.
5. Generated concepts require repeated support and information gain.
6. Generated predictive laws require held-out reproduction.
7. Later realized counterexamples demote active generated laws while preserving lineage.
8. Module learning uses outcome-specific matched-compute ablations when available.
9. Learned topology changes only from explicit pair-synergy evidence; co-occurrence is insufficient.
10. Macro cognition remains proposal-only until separately validated.
11. Causal-law authority is distinct from predictive authority and requires randomized multi-source intervention evidence plus a passing negative control.
12. Checkpoint/restore must preserve learned routing, learned topology, concept/law status, refutations, and mutation history.
13. New measurable representation axes remain proposal-only until downstream held-out/causal validation.

These gates establish bounded component behavior only. They do not establish foundation-model weight self-modification, independent organizational custody, recursive acceleration, AGI, or ASI.
